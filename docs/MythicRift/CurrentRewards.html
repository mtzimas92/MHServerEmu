Shared Rules

  • Champion Commendations use a shared account-wide weekly cap of 350 across Rift and Omega reward systems.
  • Native Rift boss loot is suppressed. The rewards above are the configured completion rewards.
  • Eligible enemies in Omega Training and Infinite Rift, and expected bosses in Boss Gauntlet, separately roll Loot/Tables/SplinterBoostTable.prototype.
  • Normal Eternity Splinter cooldown rules still apply.
  • Boss Gauntlet Eternity Splinters are protected from wave cleanup.
  • Limited claims persist through server restarts.

Points to Review

Infinite Rift commendation volume: a full weekly progression through level 45 grants 100 Hero, 150 Protector, and 135 Champion Commendations. This is intentionally shown prominently so the community can judge whether it is excessive.
Infinite Rift catch-up behavior: reaching a high level can grant every earlier unclaimed commendation entry for the current weekly period. Account-wide claims prevent the same levels from being collected again on another hero.
Omega Training XP: all Six-Infinity-Orb Chest milestones are repeatable, totaling 22 chests per complete 30-wave cycle.
Boss Gauntlet farming: XP, Infinity Chests, and Cosmic Artifact chances are repeatable per run. Boss-unique rolls are daily per hero and Champion Commendations are weekly per account.
This document describes the active source configuration. Omega Raid vendor offers are intentionally excluded because they are shop inventory rather than Rift completion rewards.
